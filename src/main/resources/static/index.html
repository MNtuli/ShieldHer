You are not alone.

ShieldHer helps you get support quickly if you are being harassed online: alert people you trust, keep their details ready, and find out how to report it.

In immediate danger, call SAPS 10111 or the GBV Command Centre 0800 428 428. ShieldHer does not replace emergency services.

What you can do

  • SOS alert
    One big button that alerts your trusted contacts, with your location if you allow it.
  • Trusted contacts
    Keep the people you trust ready to be alerted.
  • Support and reporting
    Helplines and clear steps for reporting harassment. Read it now, no account needed.

We collect only what we need. Passwords are hashed. Your location is shared only inside an alert, and only if you allow it.
